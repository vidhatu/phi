The purpose of this Obsidian vault to create a record of all previous material I have learned and understand the path to further my studies. The vault is not limited to just coursework, but includes any personal projects, or self study I have done. 

When I was in highschool, it was difficult to figure out what order I needed to take certain classes or learn certain things, but I hope this vault will be a helpful resource for other to gauge what they need to prioritize. 
## Structure
A project or a class will be one folder, which contains a project_name.req node, project_name.ov and project_name.topic_name nodes. The requirements node consists of all the "pre-reqs" needed to engage meaningfully with the folder. The overview will link to each topic and a concise summary. The topic nodes are going to be organized by a per folder basis as there is no set structure within these folders. Furthermore, topics may contain handwritten and digital notes. 

## Key Notes
- I am a student and these are my personal notes. The documents and files in here are most likely better explained elsewhere and I can be wrong. If I am and you'd like to correct me, please bring it to my attention by creating an issue. 
- While I may reference classes I have taken in highschool, I will not be adding them to the vault. This is because I do not want to get bogged down with regurgitating what I've already learned. 
- An edge between two nodes does not necessarily mean you must understand everything in the class, and the way I jump around will reflect that. 
- If this ends up becoming a resource that you rely on, I hope you use it to see what are the "pre-reqs" for projects you want to do rather than a study resource. 
- Everything in here is subject to change.
